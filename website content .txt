🔬 Page 3: Specialized Services (Comprehensive Sub-Pages)
Developer Note: Split this section into 3 deep columns or dedicated landing pages.
Section A: Advanced Vitreoretinal Care (The Center's Core)
• Diabetic Retinopathy Management: High blood sugar damages small retinal blood vessels. We perform high-contrast fundus mapping to catch leaks early and deliver protective anti-VEGF injections or retinal laser therapy.
• Retinal Detachment Surgery: A true medical emergency where the retina peels away from its baseline layer. Dr. Koteswararao performs microscopic, high-precision structural repairs to reposition the retina and preserve vision.
• Flashing Lights & Floating Spots Evaluation: Sudden black spots or lines in your vision can indicate a tear. We run urgent scans to seal tears before they turn into full detachments.
Section B: General Ophthalmology & Advanced Surgeries
• Micro-Incision Cataract Surgery (MICS): We replace cloudy natural lenses through a self-healing micro-incision. Patients experience minimal discomfort, no stitches, and rapid visual recovery with a selection of premium intraocular lenses (IOLs).
• Glaucoma Tracking & Prevention: Often called the "silent thief of sight" due to lack of early pain. We check intraocular pressures and track optic nerve health to manage conditions before permanent damage occurs.
Section C: Pediatric & Newborn Vision Care
• Retinopathy of Prematurity (ROP) Screening: Essential for babies born early (under 32 weeks) or with low birth weight. Abnormal blood vessels can scar the retina; our gentle screening ensures early interventions.
• Squint & Amblyopia (Lazy Eye): Corrective therapies, prescription lenses, or minor muscular adjustments to ensure normal, balanced vision growth in growing children.
📋 Page 4: Patient Guide & FAQ Block
[What to Expect: Your First Visit Checklist]
1. Bring Current Eyeglasses & Reports: Always bring your existing prescription cards, ongoing diabetic medical summaries, and a list of current medications.
2. The Dilation Notice: Most comprehensive eye or retina evaluations require eye drops that dilate your pupils.
	• It takes 20 to 30 minutes for the drops to work.
	• Your vision will remain blurry and sensitive to sunlight for 3 to 5 hours afterward.
	• Crucial Safety Rule: Please bring a family member or friend to drive you home. Avoid driving a car or motorbike by yourself post-dilation.

2. Deep-Dive Medical Symptom Checker (Home Page Feature)
Design Tip: A clean 3-tab layout section on the homepage where users click their symptoms to see the corresponding department.
• Tab 1: "Are you diabetic?"
	• Symptom list: Blurry zones in central vision, dark floating spots (floaters), fluctuating vision quality.
	• Action advice: You need a dilated retinal evaluation immediately. Persistent high blood sugar weakens retinal blood vessels, causing micro-aneurysms. Early laser/injection therapy halts sudden vision loss.
• Tab 2: "Is your vision getting cloudy?"
	• Symptom list: Faded color perception, glare or halos around streetlights at night, feeling like looking through a foggy window.
	• Action advice: These are textbook indicators of age-related cataracts. We specialize in stitchless, painless Micro-Incision Cataract Surgery (MICS) with premium lens implants.
• Tab 3: "Are you a parent of a newborn?"
	• Symptom list: Infant with unequal tracking, persistent watering eyes, white glare in the pupil, or born prematurely (under 32 weeks).
	• Action advice: Schedule an immediate Retinopathy of Prematurity (ROP) evaluation. Early structural screening saves infant eyesight before permanent developmental complications arise.
